Magnetic car phone mount study

简体中文

Input

Synthetic teaching case. Decide whether to fund supplier samples for a magnetic car phone mount using mock candidate listings, reviews, quotes and cost inputs.

Process

After relevance filtering, 24 mock direct candidates remain. The case separates rough-road retention, one-hand release and unresolved heat and adhesive durability. It translates customer friction into testable specifications, quote requests and sample gates.

Result

In the 1,000-unit fixture, modeled contribution margin is 22.3%, break-even ACoS is 36.3% and first-order cash is $12,550. The sample decision is conditional.

Decision

Verify real quotes and patent candidates, then run vibration, one-hand use and heat checks before committing inventory. The numbers describe the fixture assumptions.
