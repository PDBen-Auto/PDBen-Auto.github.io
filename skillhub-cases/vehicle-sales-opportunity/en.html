Vehicle Sales Opportunity

Comparable-period vehicle analysis

简体中文

Input

Synthetic teaching case. The input covers North American cars, motorcycles, UTVs, ATVs and boats. UTV data includes separate retail and wholesale series.

Process

Compare within the same country, vehicle class, metric and matched period. Preserve gaps and baseline size, with stock and flow kept separate.

Result

The synthetic output flags US Aster Canyon retail as growth at scale, with Canada flagged as small-base growth. US Demo Explorer UTV retail and wholesale receive separate results.

Decision

Use a growth-at-scale segment to form an accessory hypothesis, then verify generation, interface and customer need.
