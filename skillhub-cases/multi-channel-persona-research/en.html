Multi-Channel Persona Research

Booking tool onboarding

简体中文

Input

Synthetic teaching case. The tool must choose between creating a service first and connecting a calendar first. The four mock sources begin with 144 screened records.

Process

Within-source deduplication yields 96 evidence units: 45 about service setup, 29 about scheduling conflicts and 22 unclear or other. A valid first booking within seven days is the primary metric.

Result

Each simulated arm has 1,200 eligible accounts. A produces 384 first valid bookings (32.0%) and B 330 (27.5%). The 4.5-point lift misses the 5.5-point rollout threshold.

Decision

Keep B as the default. Validate A in the solo segment and investigate scheduling conflicts separately for teams. All figures are simulated.
