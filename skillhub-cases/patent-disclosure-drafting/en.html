Desk lamp with a replaceable light module

简体中文

Input

Synthetic teaching case. A lamp needs a replaceable light module, tilt adjustment and a clean housing. Inputs are mock sketches and inventor notes.

Process

Map the lower shell (10), insert (20), tilt bracket (30) and rear latch (40). Separate visible evidence, inventor notes and claims awaiting tests.

Result

Deliver a component table, a five-view figure register and separate technical and appearance outlines. The latch sequence and anti-glare claim remain open.

Decision

Confirm the latch motion and underside contact geometry before counsel review. Jurisdiction and claim scope require confirmation.
