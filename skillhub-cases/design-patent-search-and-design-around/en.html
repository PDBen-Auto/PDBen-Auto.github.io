Design Patent Search and Design Around

Modular wall-mounted storage rail

简体中文

Input

Synthetic teaching case. The product uses a long rounded rail, two equal hanging baskets, a central bridge and repeated horizontal openings. Preserve load capacity, one-tool installation and low landed cost.

Process

Search by product synonyms, design classes, applicant and key detail views. Compare overall silhouette, basket relationships, end returns and the opening pattern.

Result

The alternatives are a folded spine with integrated pockets, offset independent plates and cantilevered blade shelves. The folded spine changes silhouette and component rhythm while retaining mounting points.

Decision

Prioritize wall-anchor load, packaging and manufacturability checks for the spine, followed by a second search. Review visual similarity, complaints, ownership and unpublished filings separately.
